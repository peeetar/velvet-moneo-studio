Velvet Moneo is an accounting, controlling and management firm in Skopje, run by M-r Dushanka Velkovska (10+ years as a licensed accountant, 17 years as a health-practice manager). Its social posts now sell the firm as **the system behind a business**, not only as bookkeeping: numbers, processes, control and growth in one place. Discounts and prices are no longer the message.

The look comes from three posts: "Твојата фирма се подготвува…", "Една фирма за сите твои потреби" and "Ти ја имаш идејата. Ние ги имаме системите." Build every new post from them.

## Voice

- Write in Macedonian, informal **ти** for founders and owners ("Ти ја имаш идејата"). Use **Вие** only in personal posts signed by Dushanka about clinics.
- Short, declarative lines. Contrast is the main device: problem → system ("Без контролинг – одлуките се интуитивни. Со контролинг – врз основа на податоци").
- The firm speaks as **ние**; the reader owns the idea, the growth, the decisions. "Ние се грижиме за бројките. Ти се фокусираш на раст."
- Recurring words: систем, контрола, јасна слика, бројки, раст, без хаос, од првиот ден, сè на едно место.
- On the image: UPPERCASE Oswald headlines, mono uppercase labels. In captions: sentence case, line breaks between ideas, emoji only as bullets (📊 📂 🧠 🚀 ✅ ❌ 👉 🌳 💚), never mid-sentence.
- Every post ends with one action: "Пиши ни порака", "Пиши ни DM", or a comment keyword ("СТАРТ", "КОНТРОЛИНГ").
- Do not put prices, percentages off or deadlines on new posts. Old promo posts stay in the archive only.
- Never invent statistics. True numbers you can use: 10+ years of accounting, 17 years of health-practice management, one firm for registration, accounting, controlling and management.

## Color

- Start every post from one of three presets: **Olive** (`olive` ground, `forest` ink, `cream` cards), **Cream** (`cream` ground, `forest` ink, `olive` stickers), **Forest** (`forest` ground, `cream` ink, `olive` bar). The semantic tokens `ground`, `ink`, `accent`, `bar`, `on-bar` switch with the preset.
- `forest` on `olive` (6.4:1) and `forest` on `cream` (13.9:1) carry all text. `olive` text on `cream` is decorative only.
- `olive-faded` exists for one joke: words that are "deleted" (СТРЕС И ХАОС → ИЗБРИШАНИ), struck through.
- `rust` marks only the "without" side of a comparison. One rust element per post at most.
- Patterns are drawn in `ink` or `olive-light` at 8–18% opacity so headlines stay readable.

## Type

- Headlines: Oswald 700, uppercase, tight leading (`display-xl`, `display-l`). Outline variant: same face, transparent fill, 2px `ink` stroke, used for the second half of a two-part headline only.
- Labels, checklists, chips: Roboto Mono, uppercase, letter-spaced (`mono-label`, `mono-item`, `mono-chip`). The "software" tone (БИЗНИСГЕНЕРАТОР.EXE, 87%, ГОТОВО ✓) is a brand signature; use it on at least one element per post.
- The app interface uses Golos Text at 20px minimum.

## Layout

- Canvas 1080px wide: 4:5 (1350), 1:1 (1080), 9:16 (1920). Margin `post-margin` (84px) on all sides.
- Structure top to bottom: logo lockup + mono corner label → headline → one content block (progress bar, list cards, chips, quote, stat) → CTA bar.
- Everything is square-cornered (`radius-none`). Depth comes from a 1–2° tilt and `shadow-card` on cards and stickers, never from rounded corners.
- The CTA bar is full margin-width, `bar` fill, Oswald text in `on-bar`, with an `olive` ↗ arrow right-aligned.

## Imagery

- Photos are optional. When used, they get one treatment: grayscale + olive multiply (duotone) or a forest overlay at 60%, so they sit inside the palette. Subjects: desks, documents, charts, hands at work, plants (growth, the tree). No smiling-handshake clichés as the main image.
- Background patterns: dollar or денар signs, polka dots, graph paper, ledger lines, diagonal stripes, emoji repeats. One pattern per post.

## Logo

- The horizontal lockup (tree + stacked VELVET / MONEO, `assets/Logos`) sits top-left at 76px tall on a 1080px post, in `ink`. On a photo it is always `cream`.
- The Cyrillic ВЕЛВЕТ МОНЕО lockup is an equal alternative; pick one per post, never both.
- The bare tree is a pattern, not a signature: repeat it at 8–12% opacity, never place it alone as the logo.
- Never redraw, stretch, outline or add effects to the tree.

## Backgrounds

- One background treatment per post: a flat ground, one pattern, or one photo.
- Patterns: $ / € / % signs, ДЕН, polka or big dots, graph paper, ledger lines, stripes, plus signs, checkmarks, ↗ arrows, digits, mini bar charts, an emoji repeat, or the tree. Draw them in `ink` at 8–18% opacity; behind chips and checklists the chips take a solid `ground` fill.
- Photos fill the top 56% on photo layouts, or the whole post behind a 68% `forest` (or 78% `cream`) wash. The default treatment is the olive duotone (grayscale × `olive` multiply).

## Stickers

Allowed accents, one or two per post: the forest label tag, the round stamp (ВЕЛВЕТ МОНЕО • СМЕТКОВОДСТВО • КОНТРОЛИНГ around a short fact), a star badge, a paper note with honey tape, a hand-drawn arrow, a `rust` circle or underline to mark one word, a rising bar chart, a progress ring, or a single emoji.
